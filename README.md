# Tarjama

Translate anything you select on your Mac. Select text in any app, tap **Command twice** (⌘ ⌘), and a small frosted card appears right under your selection with the translation.

- Arabic becomes English and English becomes Arabic, automatically. Switch to English, العربية or Français with one click.
- **Return** copies the translation, **Replace** swaps your selection for it, **Speak** reads it aloud, **Esc** closes.
- Runs on your Mac with Apple's built-in Translation framework. Nothing is sent online.
- Without Accessibility access, use right-click › Services › Translate with Tarjama.

## Build

Needs macOS 15+ and Apple's Command Line Tools (`xcode-select --install`).

```sh
./build.sh
```

Or double-click **Build Tarjama.command**.
